Quiet, but stable

September 22, 2026 · Pinpanicaille

TL;DR
  • Axiom hasn't been very active lately, and it probably won't be for a while.
  • The good news: the app is in a pretty stable, usable state right now.
  • We just fixed the bugs that piled up over the summer, so it's a good time to try it.

Let's not pretend otherwise: the project has been pretty quiet since June. July was basically summer holidays, August had one big update and not much else. If you checked the Dev updates page and wondered whether Axiom was dead, fair question.

It's not dead. It's just slow.

What to expect

Honestly? Not a lot of activity for now, and maybe not for a while. Life happens, other projects happen. Updates will come when they come, probably in small batches rather than a steady stream.

So no big promises here. The roadmap is still there, the ideas are still there, but we're not going to pretend we'll ship something every week.

The good part

Axiom is in a pretty decent place. You can create a universe, play it, rewind, edit your saves, and the memory system does its job. It's still an early alpha (expect rough edges), but it works.

We also just went through everything that landed over the summer and fixed what broke along the way:

  • the desktop window could freeze at startup, it opens again;
  • a fresh install could choke on the bundled Myria universe, that's fixed;
  • rewinding a game now puts your inventory back too (before, you kept the sword you picked up in the future you just erased, which was a bit of a cheat);
  • the web version got a proper security pass.

So if you've been waiting for a calm moment to give it a go, this is it. Nothing is moving under your feet right now.

Still around

Found a bug, or want something? Open an issue on GitHub or come say hi on Discord. We read everything, we just answer at our own pace.

See you around.
